# AADHIRAI – Brand Identity & Design Guide

## 1. Brand Hierarchy
- **Primary Brand Name**: **AADHIRAI** (ஆதிரை)
- **Supporting Identity**: **Nammaoor AI**
- **Product Definition**: **AI-Powered Civic Accountability Engine**
- **Tagline**: *"Complaints don't end at submit. They end at proof."*
- **Tamil Tagline**: *"மக்களின் புகார் முதல் தீர்வு நிரூபணம் வரை."*

## 2. Geometric Emblem Design
The AADHIRAI emblem is a precision-engineered 4-quadrant diamond geometry:
- **Top-Left Quadrant**: Civic Emerald Teal (`#13C6B5`) representing Community, Cleanliness & Sustainability.
- **Top-Right Quadrant**: Connected Sky Cyan (`#0EA5E9`) representing Location Intelligence & Spatial GIS.
- **Bottom-Right Quadrant**: Action Royal Blue (`#075CE5`) representing Department Routing, Work Orders & Field Action.
- **Bottom-Left Quadrant**: Institutional Navy (`#082D50`) representing Municipal Governance, Authenticity & Verification.
- **Central Core**: White Keystone Diamond (`#FFFFFF`) symbolizing Transparency, Accountability, and Public Proof.

## 3. Brand Variants
Implemented in `frontend/src/components/AadhiraiBrandLogo.tsx`:
- `horizontal`: Standard emblem + bold wordmark + teal subtitle (Navbar, Login card, Landing header).
- `vertical`: Stacked emblem + centered wordmark (Splash screens, Mobile hero).
- `icon-only`: Standalone geometric diamond SVG (Favicon, app bar pills, map markers).
- `variant="white"`: Optimized for dark navy backgrounds (`#082D50`).
- `variant="dark"`: Optimized for clean white/light panels (`#FFFFFF` / `#F4F8FC`).
